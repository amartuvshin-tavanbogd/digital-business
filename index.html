    Recommend and deliver ready-to-deploy enterprise solutions to internal customers. Deliverable: completed sales and deployments.

!
Current Challenges
Standard Modules vs. Diverse Business Models

Rolling out a single multi-company ERP setup across 18 subsidiaries with very different industries (retail, finance, F&B, construction, hospitality, logistics) makes it difficult to fit all of them into the standard module set. Each company also raises a high volume of custom requirements, creating constant scope and configuration tension.

No Standardized Demand Prioritization

Development requests flow in continuously from across the Group, but there is no consistent framework to evaluate, score, and prioritize them. This results in ad-hoc decisions and difficulty aligning delivery capacity with business value.

Current Challenges
Resource Gap on Internally-Owned Platforms

For platforms not yet started, our company is the product owner itself, but there are not enough full-time resources to drive execution. This slows down kickoff and puts delivery timelines at risk.

Undefined Business Requirements

For LinkOn, HomeOn, and PayOn v3, the business requirements — product type, target users, and strategic objectives — are not yet fully defined. Without clear scope, the team cannot move into structured design and development.

!
Current Challenges
No Defined Automation Strategy

Without a clear, group-wide automation strategy, use cases are not consistently identified, prioritized, or matched to the right solution. This leads to scattered initiatives instead of a cohesive automation portfolio with measurable business outcomes.

No Unified Platform & Architecture

Automation efforts are spread across disconnected tools and patterns with no shared platform or reference architecture. This creates duplicated work, integration overhead, and limits our ability to scale automation reliably across the Group.
